# Google Play store listing graphics

| File | Play Console field | Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `play-icon-512.png` | App icon | 512×512 PNG |
| `feature-graphic.png` | Feature graphic | 1024×500 PNG |
| `screenshot-*.jpg` | Phone screenshots (also usable for 7" and 10" tablet) | 1920×1080, 16:9 landscape |

Screenshots are real in-game renders of version 1.1.0:

1. `screenshot-1-stalk.jpg`: stalking a bharal herd at dusk
2. `screenshot-2-ambush.jpg`: an ambush pounce off a ledge onto an ibex
3. `screenshot-3-wolves-blizzard.jpg`: facing a wolf pack in a blizzard
4. `screenshot-4-scent-night.jpg`: Scent Vision trails by moonlight
5. `screenshot-5-cubs.jpg`: a mother and her three cubs at their den
6. `screenshot-6-wardrobe.jpg`: coat colours and accessories in the Wardrobe
