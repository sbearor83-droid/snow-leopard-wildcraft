# Delete your Snow Leopard World account

**App:** Snow Leopard World (`com.snowleopardworld.game`) on Google Play
**Developer:** Polar Bear 23

You only have an account if you used **Sign in with Google** in the game's Cloud Save menu. If you never signed in, nothing is stored outside your phone and there is nothing to delete.

## Delete it in the game (fastest)

1. Open Snow Leopard World.
2. Tap the **☁️ Cloud Save** button.
3. Tap **Delete my cloud save** and confirm.

Your cloud save is deleted straight away and your Google sign-in record is removed from the game's account system.

## Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, or the in-game button doesn't work, email the developer at the **contact email shown on the Snow Leopard World Google Play listing** (under *App support*). Send it **from the Google account you signed in with**, with the subject **"Delete my account"**.

We will delete your account and cloud save **within 30 days** and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in record: your Google account's user ID, name and email address as held by the game.
- Your cloud save: accessories, in-game currency, daily rewards, and your leopard's level and stats.

## What is kept

- **Nothing is kept by us** after deletion. No backups of cloud saves are retained.
- **Progress saved on your phone** stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. It never left your device.
- **Advertising data** collected by Google AdMob is held by Google under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and is not linked to your game account.

Signing in again later creates a new, empty cloud save.

See the full [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
