# Privacy Policy — Snow Leopard World

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Snow Leopard World is a single-player game. You don't need an account to play, and unless you turn on cloud saves, your progress stays on your device and we collect no personal information.

## Advertising

The Android app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

- device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- app interactions with ads, and diagnostic/performance data

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google uses it as described in its privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the **Ad privacy** button in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The web version of the game shows no ads and collects nothing.

## Cloud saves (optional)

If you choose **Sign in with Google** in the game's Cloud Save menu, your progress (accessories, in-game currency, daily rewards and your leopard's level and stats) is stored in **Google Firebase** so it can be restored on a reinstall or a new device. To do this, Firebase keeps:

- your Google account's user ID, name and email address (for sign-in)
- your game progress, linked to that user ID

This data is used only to save and restore your game. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising. Google processes it as described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

You can **delete your cloud save and sign-in account at any time** with **Delete my cloud save** in the Cloud Save menu, or by opening an issue at the address below and asking us to delete it. Signing out keeps your cloud save so you can sign in again later.

The game is not directed at children under 13.
