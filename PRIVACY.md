# Privacy Policy — Snow Leopard World

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Snow Leopard World is a single-player game. We (the developer) do not run any servers, do not require an account, and do not collect any personal information ourselves.

## Advertising

The Android app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

- device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- app interactions with ads, and diagnostic/performance data

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google uses it as described in its privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the **Ad privacy** button in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The web version of the game shows no ads and collects nothing.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
