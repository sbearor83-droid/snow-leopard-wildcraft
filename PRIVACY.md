# Privacy Policy — Snow Leopard: Mountain Hunter

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Snow Leopard: Mountain Hunter is a single-player offline game.

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.** The game does not:

- require an account or sign-in
- collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers
- use analytics, advertising, or tracking services
- contain in-app purchases

All gameplay happens on your device and nothing is sent to us or to third parties.

The app declares the standard Android internet permission because it runs inside a web view (Capacitor); the game does not use it to send any data.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/sbearor83-droid/snow-leopard-wildcraft/issues
